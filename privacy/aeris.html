Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-01-18

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this application ("the App") handles user information. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data or non-personal data from users.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Personal information (such as name, email address, phone number)
  • Device information
  • Usage data
  • Location data
  • Cookies or tracking technologies
  • Analytics or advertising identifiers

All functionality of the App operates entirely without collecting any user data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services that collect data from users, including analytics tools, advertising networks, or external SDKs that track user behavior.

4. Data Sharing

Since no data is collected, there is no data to share, sell, rent, or disclose to any third parties.

5. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or store any data, there is no risk of data breaches involving user information.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Therefore, it complies with applicable children's privacy laws such as COPPA.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the revised version will be made available with an updated effective date. Any changes will reflect continued compliance with a no-data-collection approach unless explicitly stated otherwise.
